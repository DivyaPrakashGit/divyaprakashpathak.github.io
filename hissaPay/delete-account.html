HISSAPAY ACCOUNT

Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your HissaPay account and associated data.

Delete your account from HissaPay

If you are currently signed in to HissaPay, the easiest way to delete your account is directly from the application.

  • Open HissaPay.
  • Open your Profile.
  • Select Delete Account.
  • Confirm the account deletion.
  • Enter your current password when requested for security verification.

After successful verification, HissaPay deletes the Firebase Authentication account and clears the HissaPay local database on the device.

Request account deletion

If you cannot access HissaPay, you can request account deletion by contacting HissaPay support.

Please send your request from the email address associated with your HissaPay account and include the subject:

HissaPay Account Deletion Request

Send your deletion request to:

Request Account Deletion

What will be deleted?

When an authenticated HissaPay account is successfully deleted:

  • The Firebase Authentication account associated with the HissaPay user is deleted.
  • HissaPay's local database on the device is cleared.
  • Locally stored HissaPay group, member and expense information is removed from the device.

Using HissaPay as a guest?

Guest mode does not create a Firebase Authentication account.

Guest information and locally stored expenses can be removed by clearing the application's local data or uninstalling the application.

Important

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Please make sure you no longer need your HissaPay account or the associated expense information before requesting deletion.
