Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Introduction

HissaPay ("HissaPay", "we", "us", or "our") is an expense-sharing application designed to help users manage shared expenses between groups, friends and other participants.

This Privacy Policy explains what information HissaPay handles, how that information is used, how it is stored, and how users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on how you use HissaPay, the application may handle the following information:

  • Name: The name provided when creating an account or when using HissaPay as a guest.
  • Email address: The email address associated with a registered HissaPay account.
  • Authentication information: Account authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication. HissaPay does not have access to your plaintext password.
  • Group information: Information about groups created within the application.
  • Member information: Names of people added to an expense-sharing group.
  • Expense information: Expense titles, amounts, payer information, participants and related expense dates entered by the user.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

HissaPay uses local device storage to provide its expense-sharing functionality.

Group information, members and expense information are stored locally on the device using the application's local database.

Guest-mode information, including the guest name provided by the user, is also stored locally on the device.

Local expense data is used to provide the application's group and expense-management functionality.

4. Firebase Authentication

HissaPay uses Firebase Authentication to provide account registration and email/password sign-in functionality.

When you create an account, information such as your name and email address is associated with your Firebase Authentication account.

Firebase handles authentication credentials using its authentication infrastructure. HissaPay does not store or have access to your plaintext password.

5. How Information Is Used

Information handled by HissaPay is used to provide and operate the application's features, including:

  • Creating and managing HissaPay accounts.
  • Identifying the signed-in user.
  • Creating and managing expense groups.
  • Recording and calculating shared expenses.
  • Calculating balances and settlements.
  • Providing account-management functionality, including account deletion.

6. Sharing of Information

HissaPay does not sell users' personal information.

Account authentication information is processed through Firebase Authentication, a third-party service provided by Google.

We do not intentionally share users' personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

7. Data Security

HissaPay uses Firebase Authentication for account authentication and relies on the security mechanisms provided by the Android platform and Firebase services.

Users should also protect their account credentials and avoid sharing their passwords with other people.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

HissaPay retains account information for as long as the user maintains a registered account, subject to the operation of the services used by the application.

Users can permanently delete their HissaPay account from within the application.

When an authenticated account is successfully deleted through the application, the Firebase Authentication account is deleted and the HissaPay local database on the device is cleared.

Users can also request account deletion through the external account-deletion page provided on this website.

9. Guest Mode

HissaPay may be used in guest mode without creating a Firebase account.

Information entered while using guest mode, including the guest name and local expense information, is stored locally on the device.

Guest mode does not create a Firebase Authentication account.

10. Children's Privacy

HissaPay is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's features, data practices or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with the updated date shown at the top.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, HissaPay's data practices or your account, please contact the HissaPay support team.
